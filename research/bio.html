Bio

I am a full professor at Université Rennes 2, a position I have held since September 2022 after nine years as an assistant professor. I currently conduct my research at Inria Rennes within the MALT team. My work lies at the intersection of machine learning and time series, with a focus on optimal transport, differentiable programming, and time series foundation models. I also head the Master's program in Applied Mathematics and Statistics at Université Rennes 2 and teach at Institut Polytechnique de Paris (École Polytechnique).
